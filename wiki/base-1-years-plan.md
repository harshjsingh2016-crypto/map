# Base 1 — the year's plan

The goal side of the problem-discovery frame, stated as six areas of life rather than a
single outcome. In the user's words: **Work** — transform what I work on; **Money** —
cashflow and future proofing; **Mind** — spirit and focus, with Kriya; **Body** — healthy
body, good habits; **Friends and Family** — staying in touch and involved; **Home** —
smooth operations.

The board's purpose is problem discovery: finding which problems deserve time and
attention, with Base 1 as the destination those problems stand between. Each area carries
two open fields — details, and what success looks like — so that "reached" is defined per
area rather than left as a feeling about the year.
