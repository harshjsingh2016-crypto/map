# Predictive AI vs Generative AI

Traditional AI analyzes data and predicts outcomes — a score, a class, a forecast. Generative AI produces content. The shift is not a replacement: prediction is still everywhere (fraud scoring, demand forecasting, recommendations), generation now sits beside it.

The class made the split concrete with the puppy example. Show a predictive model a photo of a puppy and it identifies what it is, with a confidence score. Give the same puppy to a generative model and it can modify the image based on inputs. Same image, two different jobs: one reads it, the other rewrites it.

Generation is also multimodal, not just text. Google's stack illustrates the division of labour: Gemini understands the request and calls the others, Nanobanana generates and edits images, Veo generates video.
